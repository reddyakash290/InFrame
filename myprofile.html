About

I'm a cinematographer and DOP based in Mumbai with 6 years of experience across indie films, documentaries, music videos, and commercial productions. I'm drawn to stories that live in natural light — the grain, the imperfection, the honesty of film aesthetics even when shooting digital.

My visual language is rooted in observational documentary, but I bring that sensibility into narrative work. Currently seeking collaborations with directors working on feature films, short films, or long-form docs. Let's make something lasting.
